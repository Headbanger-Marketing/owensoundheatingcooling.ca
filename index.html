FAQs

Questions Owen Sound Homeowners Ask Us

Answers tailored to Grey County homes, weather, and heating setups.

How fast can you get to my Owen Sound home for a no-heat call?

We are based in Owen Sound and prioritize no-heat calls during cold snaps, so most homes in the valley and city see a technician the same day. Rural properties between here, Meaford, and Markdale we reach as quickly as the roads allow, with an honest ETA over the phone.

Can you convert my oil furnace to a heat pump on a rural Grey County property?

Yes. Oil-to-heat-pump conversions are a big part of what we do around Owen Sound. We remove the old tank, size a cold-climate heat pump to carry the full winter heating load, and file the rebate paperwork so you can stop paying for oil deliveries.

Do you service propane furnaces in Markdale and ski country near Blue Mountain?

Yes. Propane furnaces are standard around Markdale and Blue Mountain, and we install, tune up, and repair propane heating systems for full-time homes, weekend places, and ski-cottages alike.

I have an older heritage home in the Owen Sound valley. Can you help with uneven heating?

That is one of our specialties. Older valley homes often struggle with aging ductwork, single-zone heating, and drafty rooms. We diagnose the real cause and recommend fixes ranging from duct cleaning and zoning to a ductless mini-split in stubborn rooms.

Do you handle seasonal HVAC for Sauble Beach and Tara even though they are in Bruce County?

Yes. Sauble Beach and Tara sit in Bruce County, but they are part of our regular service area. We do cottage spring startups, summer AC repairs, fall furnace shutdowns, and year-round heat pump service on those properties.

Do you offer financing on new furnaces, heat pumps, and mini-splits?

We do. Financing is available on major equipment installs across Owen Sound and Grey County, including cold-climate heat pumps and ductless systems. Ask us about current plans when you request your free quote.
