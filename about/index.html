Built for Owen Sound Winters and Georgian Bay Summers

We are Owen Sound Heating & Cooling, a local HVAC team that has spent years keeping Grey County homes comfortable through lake-effect cold snaps, humid summers, and everything in between.

Our Story

Georgian Bay Grit, Escarpment Expertise

At Owen Sound Heating & Cooling, we believe every home on the Georgian Bay shoreline, from a century house in the valley to a farmhouse outside Chatsworth, deserves honest, dependable heat and cooling. We are based in Owen Sound, the county seat of Grey County, and we built this company around the weather and the homes that define this region.

Owen Sound is a city shaped by geography. Set in a valley along the Niagara Escarpment at the mouth of the Sydenham River, it catches heavy lake-effect snow off Georgian Bay and holds the damp cold long after the calendar says spring. Heritage homes near downtown were built for a different era of heating, and rural properties across Grey County still rely on propane and oil. That mix is exactly what we are built to service.

Our journey started with a simple goal: bring honest, transparent HVAC service to homeowners who were tired of call centres and high-pressure sales. Whether we are tuning up a gas furnace in a newer build on the hill, converting an oil system on a rural property, or cleaning decades of dust out of a heritage home's ductwork, the standard stays the same. Explain the real problem, price it clearly, and do the work right.

What We Work On Every Day

From propane furnaces in Markdale ski country to oil-to-heat-pump conversions on farms between Chatsworth and Durham, from cottage AC repairs at Sauble Beach to heritage-home duct cleaning in the valley, our team handles the heating and cooling work that keeps Grey County livable year-round.

Our Commitment

Our Commitment to Grey County Homeowners

That means propane and oil furnaces on rural properties, high-efficiency gas units in newer valley builds, cold-climate heat pumps for homeowners moving away from oil, and the ductless mini-splits that heat workshops and additions across Grey County. Our technicians carry the parts and the experience to handle all of it.
